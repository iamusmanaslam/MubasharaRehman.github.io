About

I'm a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence working on computer vision, deep learning and medical image analysis. I design generative AI models for CT and MRI reconstruction, image enhancement and cross-modality domain translation using PyTorch, and I enjoy working across academia, healthcare and industry to bring these methods into real clinical workflows. Looking ahead, I want to keep advancing AI research while exploring Neuro-AI, multimodal learning, foundation models and intelligent systems that address complex real-world challenges.

Collaborations with IRCCS CRO Aviano (Italy) · Leiden University Medical Center (Netherlands) · Philips Research · Universidad de Santiago de Compostela (Spain)

Research interests

Deep learning for medical imaging

Generative models (CNNs, Vision Transformers, diffusion, flow matching) for clinical image analysis.

Metal artifact reduction in CT

Removing metal-induced artifacts from CT scans to support accurate radiotherapy planning.

CT domain transformation

Translating kVCT to MVCT to improve image quality in radiotherapy workflows.

CT reconstruction & MRI enhancement

Structure-preserving enhancement of low-field MRI towards high-field quality.
